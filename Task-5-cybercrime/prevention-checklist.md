Stay Safe Online - Prevention checklist for college students in India







1. Use strong and unique passwords- Avoid using the same passwords for multiple accounts and create them with at least 12 characters , mixing letters , numbers and symbols.
2. Enable two factor authentication- turn it on for email, banking and social media accounts to add an extra security layer.
3. Verify links before clicking- avoid clicking on suspicious or shortened links from unknown sources.
4. Never enter your UPI PIN to \*receive\* money. If someone asks for your PIN, it is a scam.
5. Be cautious with public WiFi.
6. Check UPI and payment requests carefully- never approve a collect request from unknown people.
7. Backup important data- keep copies of all the important documents on secure cloud storage.
8. Update devices and apps regularly- install security patches and updates to protect against known vulnerability.

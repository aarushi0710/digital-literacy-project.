                         Online/UPI Payment Fraud Case Study









-What is it?

Online/UPI payment fraud refers to fraudulent activities that occur through the use
of the Unified Payments Interface (UPI), a digital payment system in India. These 
frauds include phishing, fake UPI apps, QR code frauds, and impersonation.





-What happened in the case?

A citizen approached the Central Division's Cyber Crime (CEN) police station, 
alleging fraudulent UPI transactions amounting to approximately 14.8 lakhs from his
wife's and son's private sector bank accounts. The complainant discovered the 
unauthorised transactions only recently, though they occurred between December
14,2024 and February 21,2025. According to the complaint , all the fraudulent 
transactions were conducted via UPI without the victims entering their PINs or
receiving and SMS notifications.The wife's account stood at a loss of 6,83,698 and
the son's account stood at the loss of 9,883,41.






-How it happens?

* Fake payment request links sent via SMS or WhatsApp.
* Fraudsters send QR codes claiming to send money but scanning results in money
  being deducted.
* Instant loan apps that steal banking details.
* Criminals clone SIM cards to access OTPs.





-The Scenario

   * A seller lists a used item (e.g., a laptop) on an online marketplace. The
     fraudster contacts them quickly, acting as an eager buyer.

   * The buyer claims they cannot meet in person and offers to pay immediately via
      UPI to "book" the item.

   * Instead of sending money, the fraudster sends a QR code to the seller
     via WhatsApp. They claim, "Scan this code to receive the payment in your bank
          account."

   * When the seller scans the code and enters their UPI PIN, they are actually
     authorizing a debit (payment) from their own account rather than receiving one.

   * Once the money is deducted, the fraudster blocks the seller's number and
     disappears.







-Who is usually targeted?

Often targets people who are unfamiliar with the UPI system or who fail to verify
transaction details before approving them. This mostly includes older people , 
uneducated people and teenagers/children.





-What are the consequences?

The consequence of this fraud can be severe, including financial loss, loss of 
trust in digital payment systems, and potential legal problems. Victims may face
unauthorized transactions ,loss of funds and difficulties in recovering their money
due to the complexity of the fraud . It is crucial for victims to act promptly and 
report the fraud to prevent further losses and to help authorities trace the funds.
